  • 🥬 Spinach & Leafy Greens - Iron & antioxidants

Morning Rituals

  • 🌅 Wake up before sunrise (Brahma Muhurta)

  • 🧘‍♀️ 10-15 minutes of meditation
  • 🕉️ Oil pulling with coconut oil (5 mins)
  • 🚿 Cold water splash on face
  • 🌿 Tongue scraping (Jihwa Prakshalana)

Daily Practices

  • 💧 Drink 8-10 glasses of water

Ayurvedic Herbal Teas

🌿 Tulsi Tea

Benefits: Immunity booster, stress relief

How to make: Steep fresh tulsi leaves in hot water for 5 mins

🍃 Mint Tea

Benefits: Digestion, freshness, cooling

How to make: Boil mint leaves with ginger, add honey

🟡 Turmeric Latte (Golden Milk)

Benefits: Anti-inflammatory, glowing skin

How to make: Warm milk + turmeric + black pepper + honey

🍋 Ginger Lemon Tea

Benefits: Digestion, immunity, detox

How to make: Boil ginger, add lemon juice and honey

Sleep Hygiene Tips

  • 😴 Maintain consistent sleep schedule (10 PM - 6 AM)
  • 📵 No screens 1 hour before bed
  • 🕯️ Dim lights in evening
  • 🌿 Use lavender or jasmine essential oil
  • 📖 Read a book instead of scrolling
  • 🛏️ Keep bedroom dark and cool
  • 🚫 Avoid heavy meals 2-3 hours before sleep

DIY Natural Beauty Products

🌹 Rose Water Toner

Ingredients: Fresh rose petals, distilled water

How to make: Boil petals in water, steep overnight, strain, store in spray bottle

Uses: Toner, refresher, makeup setting spray

🥥 Whipped Body Butter

Ingredients: Shea butter, coconut oil, almond oil

How to make: Melt and whip until fluffy

Uses: Deep moisturizer for dry skin

🍯 Honey & Oatmeal Scrub

Ingredients: Oats, honey, yogurt

How to make: Grind oats, mix with honey and yogurt

Uses: Gentle exfoliation for face and body

🌿 Herbal Hair Oil

Ingredients: Coconut oil, amla, brahmi, bhringraj

How to make: Heat oil with herbs, strain

Uses: Hair growth, strength, shine
