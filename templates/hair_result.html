DIY Hair Packs from Your Kitchen

🌿 Amla & Methi Pack

Ingredients: 2 tbsp amla powder, 2 tbsp methi seeds (soaked overnight), 1 tbsp curd

How to use: Grind methi seeds, mix with amla powder and curd. Apply to scalp and hair. Leave for 45 mins, wash with mild shampoo.

Benefits: Strengthens roots, reduces hair fall, adds shine

Frequency: Once a week

🥥 Coconut & Curry Leaves Oil

Ingredients: 1 cup coconut oil, handful of curry leaves

How to use: Heat oil with curry leaves until leaves crisp. Strain, cool, massage into scalp. Leave overnight.

Benefits: Prevents greying, promotes growth, nourishes scalp

Frequency: 2-3 times per week

🧅 Onion Juice for Hair Growth

Ingredients: 2 onions, 2 tbsp coconut oil

How to use: Extract onion juice, mix with coconut oil. Apply to scalp, massage gently. Leave for 30 mins, wash thoroughly.

Benefits: Rich in sulfur, stimulates follicles, promotes regrowth

Frequency: 2 times per week
